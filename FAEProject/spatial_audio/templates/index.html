Call transport

03

Spatial engine

Working: equal-power stereo panning and up to 0.6 ms interaural delay. This approximation does not implement HRTF filtering or distinguish front from back.

Implemented
04

Personal mixer

Working: per-person volume and mute, summed stereo output, and peak normalization to 0.95 to prevent clipping. Mono comparison disables directional cues.

Implemented
05

Headphone playback

Working: render, play, and download a stereo WAV. Live latency has not been measured; the future one-way target is under 150 ms.
