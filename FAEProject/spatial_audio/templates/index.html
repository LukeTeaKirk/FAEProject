UNDER THE HOOD

From voice to space.

A modular Python foundation for the full conferencing system.

01

Audio sources

Working: uploaded PCM WAV clips or generated test tones at 48 kHz. Stereo uploads are downmixed to mono.

Implemented
02

Call transport

Planned: WebRTC signaling, Opus encoding, and one isolated incoming stream per speaker. No live session is connected.

Planned
03

Spatial engine

Working: the live studio uses the browser’s HRTF panner (Web Audio API), which gives left-right and front-back cues. The offline Python renderer uses equal-power panning and up to 0.6 ms interaural delay (no front-back cues).

Implemented
04

Personal mixer

Working: per-person volume, mute and focus, summed stereo output with a limiter, and loudness matching between mono and spatial so the comparison is fair. Mono disables directional cues.

Implemented
05

Headphone playback

Working: live playback in the browser with instant changes and left/right level meters; offline render and WAV download. Network latency has not been measured; the future one-way target is under 150 ms.

Implemented
✓

Self-test

Measures the spatial engine in this browser: ear level differences, mirror symmetry, ear-to-ear delay and front-back difference.
